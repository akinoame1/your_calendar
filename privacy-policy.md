# MyCalendar — Privacy Policy

_Effective 2026-10-09. Applies to the MyCalendar app published on Google Play._

MyCalendar is a home-screen calendar widget and app. It shows the calendars that your phone
already syncs, and lets you create and edit events and tasks in them.

## What the app accesses, and why

- **Your calendars and events.** The app needs the Android calendar permission to show your events
  on the widget, and to create, edit, move, complete and delete the events you choose. The calendar
  data stays on your phone. Your phone's own calendar sync (for example Google Calendar) carries
  your changes to your account, as it does for any calendar app.
- **Notifications and exact alarms.** These are used only to remind you of events and tasks at the
  time you chose.
- **Battery level and charging state.** Read on the phone only, to draw them on the widget.

## What leaves your phone

**Nothing, unless you send it yourself.** The Play version of MyCalendar has no internet
permission. It has no analytics, ads, tracking or accounts of its own, and the developer receives
no data from it.

Two features hand content to another app, only when you tap them:

- **Share** (for example, a question to an AI assistant, or a bug report) opens Android's share
  sheet with the text, and a picture of the widget if you include one. Where it goes after that is
  up to you and the app you pick, under that app's own privacy policy.
- **Open in Google Calendar** opens the event in Google's app.

## Data retention and deletion

The app keeps its settings and a short debug log on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes
them. Your events remain in your calendars, where you manage them as usual.

## Children

MyCalendar is not directed at children under 13.
