# What changed — version 1.0.309 (since 1.0.308)

If you read the manual for 1.0.308, this is what to read again; the check word is now **ember-328f**.

## Settings added

- `look.space.chips` — The gap under each chip, between one chip and the next
- `look.space.days` — The space above and below the line between two days
- `look.space.chip.side` — The space inside a chip, at its left and right ends
- `look.space.dayColumn` — The gap between the day column and the chips

## look-keys.md

Added: Look spaces — every look.space.* key takes a whole percent of today's space, 0..300 (0 for none); 100 on a fresh install.
