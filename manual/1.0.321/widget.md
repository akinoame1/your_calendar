# The widget — version 1.0.321

THE WIDGET, top to bottom (part names in brackets are the names look.* and look.size.* keys use):
- The page: a dark rounded panel (widget.background). Words on an empty or unreadable list (widget.text).
- The battery, by widget.battery; it is redrawn every 15 minutes (5 while charging), or at once on every change with widget.batteryLive on, which keeps a silent notification. BAR is a thin bar across the very top, filled to the charge (batteryBar.fill, one colour per charge level: fullCharge, regular, warning, critical; batteryBar.track the empty part; batteryBar.cells the gaps cutting it in quarters; batteryBar.height its thickness). PILL draws the battery inside the date button instead, see below. OFF draws none. The charge levels: fullCharge from widget.batteryFullChargeAt (80 %), regular, warning at or below widget.batteryWarningAt (30 %), critical at or below widget.batteryCriticalAt (15 %) -- numbers as shipped, CURRENT has his.
- The header row: on the left the date button, then the battery's percentage and temperature, then a small upload icon (not changeable by chat), and on the right the time and the app version (header.text). Percentage (battery.percent) and temperature (battery.temperature.cool, battery.temperature.warm from widget.batteryWarmAt, battery.temperature.hot from widget.batteryHotAt; 35° and 40° unless set) share one size (battery.text).
- The date button names the day the widget is showing; tapping it opens a month picker over the home screen, and the day picked moves the widget there. Normally it is a filled rounded box (date.fill) with words on it (date.text; the date size scales both). With the battery as PILL the button becomes an AA battery: a rounded outline (batteryDate.outline) drawn OVER a fill that rises from the left to the charge (batteryDate.fill, per charge level), the empty rest (batteryDate.track), the outline cut at the quarters into cells (batteryDate.cuts), and a band and + terminal at the right end (batteryDate.terminal). The date's words sit on top of all of it.
- Then the list of days, one section per day. On the left a day column (day.column is its width): the weekday's name (day.weekday) over the day's number (day.number); weekends and holidays in red (day.weekend), observances softer (day.observance); today's number in a round mark (today.fill, today.text; today sizes both), and today's weekday name in the same colour as the mark. Small dots under the number mark special days (day.specials). Under each day's number a + (plus) starts a new event on that day.
- Beside the day column, the day's chips: one rounded box per event or task, filled with its calendar's colour (or one softened lightness for all, widget.chipColours MUTED). A chip's words (chip.text) are the title, and for a timed event the hours on a second line (chip.time). A task's chip starts with a tick box (chip.check); a done task is drawn ticked. A chip with an alarm shows a bell (chip.alarm). A colour dot at the chip's right end (chip.handle) opens a colour choice for that item. An invitation not yet accepted is drawn as an outline instead of a filled box. Tasks come first in a day, then all-day events, then timed events by start.
- A line separates days and stands before a month (rule.day); a month's name heads its first day (month.text). Within a day, an optional line splits the morning's events from the afternoon's at widget.middayRuleHour (rule.midday, with its hour as midday.text).
- A day with nothing on it is drawn like any other day, with the words "Nothing here" in a box where its chips would be (empty.fill, empty.text).
- At the ends of the list, double arrows page to later or earlier days (nav.mark), with words such as "earlier" and "N more days" (more.text).
- Unfinished tasks from past days are carried onto today until done; events that have ended leave the list after widget.lingerMinutes. A quiet phone notification comes widget.comingUpMinutes before every timed event.

PARAMETERS above the parts: the parts drawn in one colour share a parameter, so one key recolours them all. palette.accent (the date button, today's mark, the battery's outline in the date, the paging arrows and words); palette.onAccent (words on the accent); palette.text (main words); palette.muted (quiet words); palette.line (lines and quiet fills); battery.fill (the battery's colour per charge level, everywhere: the bar, the battery in the date, and the percentage, which follows the bar). Each part keeps its own tuned colour until its parameter is set; setting the part itself overrides the parameter for that part alone.

CANCELLED: an event or task whose title starts with one of behaviour.cancelled.words (CANCELED, CANCELLED unless set) is cancelled but kept: it never notifies, rings or carries over; behaviour.cancelled.show draws it AS_IS, STRUCK or HIDDEN, and the chip.cancelled state styles it. The event screen's menu has Cancel (keep it) and Uncancel.
